Meet the guy behind it

Experience with a Hawaiian shirt, not a sales script.

I’m Mark — retiring IT senior manager, veteran, former liveaboard sailor, and longtime cruiser. I’ve spent years around ships, ports, travel decisions, and the kind of details that sound small until they cost you time, money, or patience.

This site and channel are built to help people cruise smarter while still having fun. The goal is not to act fancy. The goal is to help you enjoy the trip and avoid dumb mistakes.
