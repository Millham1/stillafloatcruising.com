# Still Afloat Brand Guide

## Brand Identity
Still Afloat is a cruise and travel lifestyle brand centered around:
- Real cruise experience
- Veteran perspective
- Smart planning
- Humor and personality
- Tropical escapism
- Authenticity over hype

The brand should feel:
- Experienced
- Relaxed
- Approachable
- Premium but not luxury-snobbish
- Fun without becoming chaotic

## Brand Voice
### Primary Tone
Conversational expert.

The audience should feel like they are talking to:
- a seasoned cruiser
- a trusted friend
- somebody who has actually done this repeatedly
- somebody who tells the truth about cruising

### Avoid
- fake hype
- overexcited influencer behavior
- corporate travel-agent language
- cruise elitism
- constant sales pressure

### Preferred Communication Style
Use:
- practical advice
- humor naturally
- observational commentary
- concise storytelling
- honest opinions

## Visual Language
### Overall Style
“Tropical premium.”

Blend:
- Caribbean blues
- sunset golds
- cruise ship whites
- clean modern layouts
- subtle nautical influence

Avoid:
- pirate clichés
- excessive anchors/wheels/palm-tree overload
- loud neon tourism styling

## Typography
### Primary Fonts
Body:
- Baloo 2

Headings:
- Bree Serif

Accent Script:
- Pacifico

### Typography Rules
- Large confident headlines
- Rounded approachable body text
- Script only for accent/logo usage
- Avoid using more than 3 font families

## Color Palette
### Core Colors
Deep Navy:
#07183f

Ocean Blue:
#0077b6

Seafoam:
#5dff9a

Sun Gold:
#ffca4f

Cloud White:
#f7fbff

### Usage
- Navy for structure and authority
- Blue for energy and cruise/ocean identity
- Seafoam for accents/highlights
- Gold for calls-to-action and warmth

## Logo Guidance
### Important Rendering Decision
DO NOT use:
- -webkit-text-stroke

Reason:
- caused double-render blur artifacts

Preferred approach:
- layered text shadows
- gradient clipping
- crisp rendering

## Thumbnail Strategy
### Established Direction
- Face positioned right
- Ship positioned left
- Minimal bold text
- High emotional clarity
- No clutter
- No tiny unreadable text

### Emotional Hooks
Use:
- surprise
- humor
- concern
- excitement
- curiosity

Avoid:
- clickbait lies
- fake outrage
- overcrowded thumbnails

## Website Style
### Layout Philosophy
Prioritize:
- readability
- breathing room
- large visuals
- mobile friendliness
- modular sections

### Preferred Effects
- subtle glows
- gradients
- smooth hover effects
- soft shadows
- glassmorphism used sparingly

Avoid:
- excessive animation
- flashy transitions
- visual overload

## Content Philosophy
### Core Themes
- Cruise planning
- Cruise reality vs marketing
- Cruise tips
- Budget optimization
- Loyalty programs
- Cabin strategy
- Travel gear
- Cruise news
- Veteran travel perspective

### Audience
Primary audience:
- adults 35+
- cruise-curious travelers
- repeat cruisers
- value-conscious travelers
- retirees/pre-retirees

## Production Standards
### Video
Preferred feel:
- cinematic but authentic
- stable footage
- smooth transitions
- natural lighting when possible

### Music
Preferred inspiration:
- Jimmy Buffett
- Kenny Chesney
- Zac Brown Band coastal energy

Avoid:
- heavy EDM
- cheesy royalty-free ukulele overload
- excessive steel drum stereotypes

## Long-Term Goal
Still Afloat should evolve into:
- a recognizable cruise media brand
- a trusted cruise planning resource
- a sustainable affiliate business
- a travel consultation funnel
- a personality-driven cruise community
