# Still Afloat Content Strategy

## Core Mission
Help people cruise smarter while being entertained by authentic experiences and commentary.

The content should:
- educate
- entertain
- build trust
- support affiliate monetization
- build long-term audience loyalty

## Main Content Pillars
### 1. Cruise Planning
Topics:
- choosing cruise lines
- cabin selection
- budgeting
- loyalty strategies
- dining planning
- excursion planning
- embarkation/disembarkation tips

Goal:
Position Mark as an experienced cruise strategist.

## 2. Cruise Reality Content
Topics:
- what cruise lines do well
- what cruise lines hide
- mistakes people make
- realistic expectations
- crowd management
- hidden fees

Goal:
Build authenticity and trust.

## 3. Cruise Vlogs
Style:
- cinematic but real
- personality-driven
- observational humor
- immersive atmosphere

Include:
- B-roll
- narration
- environmental audio
- practical commentary

## 4. Cruise Gear
Topics:
- travel gear
- cruise cabin products
- filming equipment
- luggage strategy
- organization products

Goal:
Support affiliate monetization naturally.

## 5. Cruise News and Commentary
Topics:
- itinerary changes
- cruise industry updates
- weather impacts
- ship repositioning
- loyalty program changes

Goal:
Create recurring engagement opportunities.

## Publishing Strategy
### YouTube Longform
Target:
1-2 videos per week initially.

Focus:
- quality over quantity
- personality consistency
- watchability

### YouTube Shorts
Purpose:
- discovery
- quick tips
- humor
- teaser clips

Goal:
3-5 shorts weekly.

## Website Integration
Website should support:
- embedded videos
- affiliate recommendations
- searchable resources
- blog/news support
- lead generation

## Affiliate Strategy
Affiliate products should feel:
- genuinely useful
- experience-tested
- naturally integrated

Avoid:
- spammy product dumps
- random unrelated products

## Visual Consistency
All content should maintain:
- tropical premium color palette
- recognizable typography
- consistent thumbnail structure
- recognizable voice

## Growth Strategy
### Initial Growth Focus
Prioritize:
- trust
- consistency
- authenticity
- niche authority

Do NOT prioritize:
- rage bait
- fake controversy
- viral gimmicks

## Long-Term Expansion
Potential future areas:
- group cruises
- travel consulting
- live streams
- memberships
- digital products
- cruise guides
- sponsor partnerships
- travel agency growth

## Success Metrics
Primary:
- returning viewers
- audience trust
- affiliate conversion quality
- watch time
- engagement quality

Secondary:
- raw subscriber count
- vanity metrics
